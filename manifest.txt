Name                 "Sci-Fi Security"
Author               Knight
Description          "Laser fences and laser doors. Three fence styles, each in red or blue beams over grass, dirt, stone or sand, that connect to walls and to each other like any other fence. Single and double Laser Doors in red or blue that open for any member of staff who walks up and never for a prisoner, A Laser Door Panel holds them open while a guard stands at it, and a triggered Door Servo opens them too. One AI Laser Controller anywhere in the prison makes every Laser Door automatic, with no guard needed, and seals a door against a misbehaving prisoner. A Laser Door Wire Box on a door wires it to a Door Control System, pressure pad or door timer the way a Door Servo would, without looking like one. During a bangup a guard feeds the prisoners behind a Laser Door through it, without going inside. The fences live in the game's tileset, which Sci-Fi Objects re-materials and ships an identical copy of, so the two work together in either load order."
