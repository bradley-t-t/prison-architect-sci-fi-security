Author               Knight
Description          "Laser fences in three styles, and laser doors that let your staff through and hold prisoners back. Build one AI Laser Controller and every door runs itself, sealing the moment a prisoner beside it starts trouble.\r\n\r\n[h2]How to use it[/h2]\r\n[olist]\r\n[*]Subscribe, turn the mod on in Extras > Mods, and put it below any other mod that adds walls, floors or fences in your load order.\r\n[*]Fences: open the materials menu and pick a Laser Fence, Laser Grid Fence or Laser Curtain Fence, in red or blue, over grass, dirt, snow, stone, sand or concrete. Draw it like any fence. It joins to walls and to other fences.\r\n[*]Doors: open the objects menu and place a Laser Door ($600) or a Large Laser Door ($900), red or blue, in a doorway like any door. For a ready-made block, Quick Build has a Laser Cell Wing of 20 cells and a Laser Cell Block of 500.\r\n[*]To open doors from a post, build a Laser Door Panel ($400) within three tiles of them. While a guard stands at it, every Laser Door in reach opens.\r\n[*]To wire a door, build a Laser Door Wire Box ($200) on it and connect a Door Control System, a pressure pad or a door timer with the Connect tool, as you would a Door Servo. The box needs power and the Remote Access research.\r\n[*]To make every door automatic, build one AI Laser Controller ($5,000) anywhere.\r\n[/olist]\r\n\r\n[h2]How the doors behave[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]Without a controller, a Laser Door is a locked door. Guards open it with their keys, and it opens itself for staff who carry none, such as cooks and workmen. It shuts behind them, and prisoners wait for staff to let them through.\r\n[*]With a controller, every Laser Door is automatic. It opens for staff and for prisoners going about their day and shuts behind them. The game only sends people where they are allowed to go, so prisoners still stay out of staff only areas.\r\n[*]A door seals the moment a prisoner beside it is fighting, rioting or escaping. For a few seconds after, it is a locked door that staff with keys can still open.\r\n[*]A triggered Door Servo opens a Laser Door too.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]What changes in your game[/h2]\r\n[list]\r\n[*]A Laser Door stuns whoever attacks it, the way a tazer would drop them, and nobody can break one down.\r\n[*]During a bangup the game brings no meals to cells. A Laser Door on a cell calls a guard, who passes the meal through without going inside. Prisoners on lockdown or in solitary are still fed by the game's own guards.\r\n[/list]\r\n\r\n[h2]Conflicts[/h2]\r\nThe fences live in the game's tileset image, and the game keeps only one tileset: the one from the last mod in your load order that ships one. Put Sci-Fi Security below any other mod that adds walls, floors or fences, and expect that mod's own tiles to stop drawing. Mods that add no tiles are fine. Sci-Fi Objects ships an identical copy of this tileset, so the two work together in either load order."
