Name                 "Sci-Fi Security"
Author               Trenton
Description          "Laser fences and laser doors. Three fence styles, each in red or blue beams over grass, dirt, stone or sand, that connect to walls and to each other like any other fence. Single and double Laser Doors in red or blue that open for any member of staff who walks up and never for a prisoner, and can be opened from a Laser Door Panel while a guard stands at it or by a triggered Door Servo. One AI Laser Controller anywhere in the prison makes every Laser Door automatic, with no guard needed, and seals a door against a misbehaving prisoner. A Laser Door Wire Box on a door wires it to a Door Control System, pressure pad or door timer the way a Door Servo would, without looking like one. A prisoner locked in behind a Laser Door is fed through it by a guard who never goes inside."
